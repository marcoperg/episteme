#+title: Dependent Choice

The axiom of dependent choice is a weak form of [[id:E4068E5E-6D76-4C89-B4EC-22E84E23478F][the axiom of choice]] that is still sufficient to develop much of real analysis.


* Statement

A reletation on a set $X$ is called total if for every $a\in X$ there exists some $b\in X$ such that $a R b$.

The axioms of depedent choice states that for every nonempty set $X$ and every total relation $R$ on $X$, there exists a sequence $(x_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ in $X$ such that
\[ x_n R x_{n+1} \quad \forall \n \in\mathbb{N}\]


* Use

Wihtout this axiom one can construct inductively the first $n$ terms of the sequence, the axiom is needed in order to form a whole countbly infinite sequence.

DC is the axiom of AC that is required to show the existence of a sequence constructed by transfinite recursion of countable length, if it is necessary to make a choice at each step and if some of those choices cannot be made independently of previous choices.
