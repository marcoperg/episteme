#+title: Logic Program Semantics
#+setupfile: ../../../setup.org

* Normal programs

Rules:

$$
a \leftarrow b_1,\ldots,b_m,\operatorname{not}c_1,\ldots,\operatorname{not}c_n.
$$

* Supported semantics

$M$ is supported iff $M\models P$ and

$$
\forall a\in M\;\exists(a\leftarrow B)\in P:\ M\models B.
$$

Equivalent (propositional case) to models of Clark completion.

Allows circular support:
#+begin_src prolog
p :- q.
q :- p.
#+end_src

Supported models: $\emptyset,{p,q}$.

* Stable semantics

For $M$, the GL reduct $P^M$ is obtained by:

1. deleting rules containing $\operatorname{not}a$ with $a\in M$;
2. deleting remaining negative literals.

Then

$$
M\text{ stable}\iff M=LM(P^M).
$$

Hence every stable model is supported, but not conversely.

Example:
#+begin_src prolog
p :- not q.
q :- not p.
#+end_src

Stable models:

$$
\{p\},\qquad\{q\}.
$$

Positive self-support is rejected:
#+begin_src prolog
p :- p.
#+end_src

Unique stable model: $\emptyset$.

* Well-founded semantics

Unique three-valued interpretation

$$
WFM(P):Atoms\to\{\mathbf t,\mathbf f,\mathbf u\}.
$$

It combines consequence propagation with *unfounded-set* reasoning.

If $U$ has no support independent of $U$, atoms in $U$ are assigned $\mathbf f$.

Thus:
#+begin_src prolog
p :- p.
#+end_src

gives

$$
p=\mathbf f.
$$

For
#+begin_src prolog
p :- not q.
q :- not p.
#+end_src

$$
p=q=\mathbf u.
$$

For every stable model $M$,

$$
WFM(P)\leq_k M.
$$

Thus:

* $WFM(P)(a)=\mathbf t \Rightarrow a$ belongs to every stable model;
* $WFM(P)(a)=\mathbf f \Rightarrow a$ belongs to none;
* $\mathbf u$ means WFS does not decide.

If $WFM(P)$ is total, it is the unique stable model.

* Kripke--Kleene semantics

Least fixpoint of the three-valued immediate-consequence operator:

$$
KK(P)=\operatorname{lfp}_{\leq_k}(\Phi_P).
$$

It performs three-valued propagation but no unfounded-set inference.

Hence:
#+begin_src prolog
p :- p.
#+end_src

gives

$$
KK(P)(p)=\mathbf u,\qquad WFM(P)(p)=\mathbf f.
$$

* Summary

| Semantics | Characterization                                      |
|-----------+-------------------------------------------------------|
| Supported | Every true atom has a satisfied supporting rule       |
| Stable    | $M=LM(P^M)$; rejects unfounded/self-support           |
| WFS       | Canonical 3-valued approximation using unfounded sets |
| KK        | Least 3-valued propagation fixpoint                   |

Relations:

$$
Stable(P)\subseteq Supported(P),
\qquad
KK(P)\leq_k WFM(P)\leq_k M
$$

for every stable model $M$.
