* About these notes

These notes were taken during the Cryptographic Protocols and Applications
class of the Formal Methods for Computer Science master's program in the
2026/27 academic year.

Most lectures are based on D. Boneh and V. Shoup's /A Graduate Course in
Applied Cryptography/, which is freely available at
[[https://toc.cryptobook.us][toc.cryptobook.us]].

* Covered material

Lectures 1--3 progress from perfect secrecy to computational
indistinguishability, pseudorandom generators, one-way assumptions, stretch
amplification, and applications to commitments and stream encryption.
